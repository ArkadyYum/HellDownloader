============================================================
  HellDownloader — Universal HTML5 Video & Fragment Clipper
============================================================

SUPPORTED OS & BROWSERS:
- Windows 10 / Windows 11 (x64)
- Works in ALL Chromium browsers:
  Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Yandex Browser, Brave, Opera / Opera GX, Vivaldi

INSTALLATION (1 minute, zero manual dependencies):
1. Double-click "install.bat".
   - It automatically checks for (and downloads if missing) portable Node.js, media engine, and FFmpeg into C:\Users\Public\HellDownloader\bin.
   - It registers the local Native Host for Chrome, Edge, Yandex Browser, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi.
2. Open your browser and go to:
   chrome://extensions/
3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (Режим разработчика) in the top-right corner.
4. Click "Load unpacked" (Загрузить распакованное расширение) and select:
   C:\Users\Public\HellDownloader\extension

USAGE:
- Open any webpage with an HTML5 video player.
- Press Shift+F8 (or click the HellDownloader icon in the browser toolbar).
- Choose "Full Video" or "Cut Fragment".
- Drag the green (S) and red (E) sliders directly on the video player's timeline bar, or click any timeline chapter/episode to auto-select it.
- Step by 1 second (1s) or 1 frame (1f), pick your resolution (up to 4K or MP3 Audio Only), and click "Download".

UNINSTALLATION:
- Run "uninstall.bat" and remove the extension from chrome://extensions/.
